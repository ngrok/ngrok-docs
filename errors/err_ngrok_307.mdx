## What does ERR_NGROK_307 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_307 with this message:

```text
You must reserve an address for your account before it can be used.
Failed to create the address <ADDR> for the account <ACCOUNT> in region <REGION>.
(Hint: Did you reserve the address in this region?)

Reserve an address on your dashboard: https://dashboard.ngrok.com/tcp-addresses
```
