## What does ERR_NGROK_23005 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_23005 with this message:

```text
You've reached the maximum number of vaults (<MAX>) under your current plan. To increase your limit, upgrade your plan at https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing.
```
