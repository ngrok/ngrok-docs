## What does ERR_NGROK_AI_GATEWAY_BEDROCK_KEY_ROLE_DENIED mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_AI_GATEWAY_BEDROCK_KEY_ROLE_DENIED with this message:

```text
AWS refused to let ngrok assume the IAM role of Bedrock key <KEYID>. The role must exist and trust ngrok's principal with the key's external ID.
```
