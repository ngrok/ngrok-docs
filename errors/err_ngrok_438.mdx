## What does ERR_NGROK_438 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_438 with this message:

```text
Invalid '_' in domain name. Valid domains may not contain underscores. Failed to reserve <DOMAIN>.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_438?
