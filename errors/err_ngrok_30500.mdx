## ERR_NGROK_30500
Restarting certificate provisioning for a wildcard domain would change its ACME challenge record. Contact support instead.




## Further help
If you're having trouble resolving this error, please reach out to [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_30500)
