## What does ERR_NGROK_4108 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_4108 with this message:

```text
Sign ups are disallowed for the email provider "<DOMAIN>". Sign up with a different email provider.
```

<Tip>
Troubleshoot ERR_NGROK_4108 with your AI agent.
Connect it to the [ngrok MCP server](/mcp-server), then ask what caused the error and how to fix it.
</Tip>

## Why does ngrok return ERR_NGROK_4108?

If you're receiving this error, ngrok does not permit sign-ups from your email provider. ngrok limits sign-ups from some providers to curb abuse. To sign up for ngrok, use a different email address.
