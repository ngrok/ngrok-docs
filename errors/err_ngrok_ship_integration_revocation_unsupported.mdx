## What does ERR_NGROK_SHIP_INTEGRATION_REVOCATION_UNSUPPORTED mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_SHIP_INTEGRATION_REVOCATION_UNSUPPORTED with this message:

```text
ngrok cannot revoke a <PROVIDERID> credential at the provider. Revoke it in the provider console, then delete the integration.
```
