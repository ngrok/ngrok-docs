## What does ERR_NGROK_6622 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_6622 with this message:

```text
You may not deactivate the last administrator of the account. Grant administrator privileges to another member first.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_6622?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_6622) and include the error code.
