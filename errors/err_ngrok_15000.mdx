## What does ERR_NGROK_15000 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_15000 with this message:

```text
You are not allowed to create endpoints with subdomains on <BASE>. Your account is on the <PLAN> plan, which doesn't allow starting endpoints on <BASE>.
```
