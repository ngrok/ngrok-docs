## What does ERR_NGROK_6600 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_6600 with this message:

```text
The account <NAME> has reached its team size limit and may not add additional users.
Ask the account owner to upgrade your plan: https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing/choose-a-plan
```
