## What does ERR_NGROK_1006 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_1006 with this message:

```text
There was a problem with your credit card: <MSG>
```



## Why does ngrok return ERR_NGROK_1006?

ngrok could not process your card.

- Your card provider or bank may have declined your payment.
- ngrok accepts most major payment cards, but does not accept:
  - PayPal
  - Venmo
  - Cryptocurrency

## How do I fix ERR_NGROK_1006?

- Confirm that you're using a valid credit card.
- Confirm that your billing information is correct.
- If you're using a pre-paid card, confirm that your account balance is sufficient.

If you are repeatedly seeing this error, your bank or card provider may be able to provide additional information. ngrok support often can't do much more, but you can email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com) to ask for further investigation.
