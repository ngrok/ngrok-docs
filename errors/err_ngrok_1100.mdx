## What does ERR_NGROK_1100 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_1100 with this message:

```text
No public keys were sent for this SSH tunnel.
Configure SSH with public key authentication and register your keys at https://dashboard.ngrok.com/ssh-keys.
```
