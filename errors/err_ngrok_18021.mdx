## What does ERR_NGROK_18021 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_18021 with this message:

```text
You have exceeded the maximum number of concurrent endpoints allowed for this account. Log into https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing to review your usage and https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing/choose-a-plan to view your subscription options.
```
