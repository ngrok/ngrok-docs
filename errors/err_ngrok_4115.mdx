## What does ERR_NGROK_4115 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_4115 with this message:

```text
The email domain "<DOMAIN>" does not allow account creation. Contact your ngrok administrator or support@ngrok.com for access.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_4115?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_4115) and include the error code.
