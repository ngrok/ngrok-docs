## What does ERR_NGROK_418 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_418 with this message:

```text
Your account is limited to <MAX> wildcard domains. Email sales@ngrok.com to purchase additional wildcard domains.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_418?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_418) and include the error code.
