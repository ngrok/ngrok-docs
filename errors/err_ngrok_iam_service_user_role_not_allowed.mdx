## What does ERR_NGROK_IAM_SERVICE_USER_ROLE_NOT_ALLOWED mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_IAM_SERVICE_USER_ROLE_NOT_ALLOWED with this message:

```text
A service user can't hold the role <ROLE>. Allowed roles: developer, developer-readonly, team-manager.
```
