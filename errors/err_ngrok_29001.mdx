## What does ERR_NGROK_29001 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_29001 with this message:

```text
You don't have permission to perform this action. Your credentials are missing the <SCOPE> scope.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_29001?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_29001) and include the error code.
