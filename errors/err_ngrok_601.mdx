## What does ERR_NGROK_601 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_601 with this message:

```text
This credential is your tunnel token and cannot be deleted, only rotated.

Credential ID: <ID>
Description: <DESCRIPTION>
```

<Tip>
Troubleshoot ERR_NGROK_601 with your AI agent.
Connect it to the [ngrok MCP server](/mcp-server), then ask what caused the error and how to fix it.
</Tip>

## Why does ngrok return ERR_NGROK_601?

You tried to delete the default authtoken for your account, which ngrok calls your tunnel token.
You can't delete this authtoken, but you can rotate it.

## How do I fix ERR_NGROK_601?

To rotate your default authtoken:

1. Go to [Your Authtoken in the dashboard](https://dashboard.ngrok.com/get-started/your-authtoken).
1. Click **Reset Authtoken**.
1. Confirm the reset.
   ngrok revokes the old authtoken and shows you a new one.
1. Update every agent, SDK, and Kubernetes Operator that used the old authtoken.
   For the ngrok agent, run `ngrok config add-authtoken <NEW_AUTHTOKEN>`.

Agents, SDKs, and Kubernetes Operators that still use the old authtoken can't connect after the reset, and you can't undo it.

To manage authtokens you can delete, [create additional authtokens](https://dashboard.ngrok.com/authtokens) or use the [Credentials API](/api-reference/credentials/create).
