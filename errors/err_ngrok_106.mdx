## ERR_NGROK_106

{/* vale off */}
The authtoken you specified is an ngrok v1 authtoken, but you're using ngrok v2.
Instructions to install your authtoken are on your ngrok dashboard:
https://dashboard.ngrok.com/get-started/your-authtoken
{/* vale on */}
