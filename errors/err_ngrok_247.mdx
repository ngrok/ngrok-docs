## What does ERR_NGROK_247 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_247 with this message:

```text
The account <ACCOUNT> has been suspended for non-payment. Update your payment method to pay your outstanding bill and resume service: https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing
```
