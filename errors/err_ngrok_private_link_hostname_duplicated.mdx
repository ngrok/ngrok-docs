## What does ERR_NGROK_PRIVATE_LINK_HOSTNAME_DUPLICATED mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_PRIVATE_LINK_HOSTNAME_DUPLICATED with this message:

```text
Hostname <HOSTNAME> appears more than once in 'hostnames'. Hostnames don't depend on case.
```
