## What does ERR_NGROK_407 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_407 with this message:

```text
Domain part <PART> has an invalid first character. Valid domain parts must begin with a-z, A-Z, 0-9 or *. Failed to reserve <DOMAIN>.
```
