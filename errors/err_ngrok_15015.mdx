## What does ERR_NGROK_15015 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_15015 with this message:

```text
The credential ACL policy doesn't permit a Traffic Policy on agent endpoints. Remove the Traffic Policy, or attach it to a cloud endpoint instead.
Credential ID: <ID>
```
