## ERR_NGROK_318

{/* vale off */}
You must reserve a wildcard domain for your account before you can use it to create endpoints.
Failed to create an endpoint with the wildcard domain `DOMAIN` for the account `ACCOUNT`.

Reserve a domain on your dashboard: https://dashboard.ngrok.com/domains/new
{/* vale on */}
