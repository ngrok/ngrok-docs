## What does ERR_NGROK_419 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_419 with this message:

```text
Only Pay-as-you-go accounts can reserve wildcard domains.

Upgrade to a Pay-as-you-go plan at https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing/choose-a-plan?plan=paygo
```
