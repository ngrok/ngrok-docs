## What does ERR_NGROK_430 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_430 with this message:

```text
Another account is running an active tunnel on the domain <DOMAIN>. You may not reserve domains that are actively in use.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_430?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_430) and include the error code.
