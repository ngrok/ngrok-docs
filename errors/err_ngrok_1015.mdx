## What does ERR_NGROK_1015 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_1015 with this message:

```text
You are on a legacy custom subscription. Contact support@ngrok.com for help with changing your subscription.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_1015?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_1015) and include the error code.
