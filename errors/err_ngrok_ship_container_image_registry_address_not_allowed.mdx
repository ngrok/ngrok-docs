## ERR_NGROK_SHIP_CONTAINER_IMAGE_REGISTRY_ADDRESS_NOT_ALLOWED

{/* vale off */}
Registry `HOST` resolves to a private or reserved address. ngrok cannot pull from it.
{/* vale on */}
