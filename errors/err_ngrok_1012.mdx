## What does ERR_NGROK_1012 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_1012 with this message:

```text
You may not purchase fewer licenses than users. Your account currently has <USERS> users.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_1012?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_1012) and include the error code.
