## What does ERR_NGROK_1202 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_1202 with this message:

```text
You can't enable Google OAuth without first connecting a Google Apps login.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_1202?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_1202) and include the error code.
