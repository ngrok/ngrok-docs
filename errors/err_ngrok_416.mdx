## What does ERR_NGROK_416 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_416 with this message:

```text
You may not register a wildcard for all <SUFFIX> domains. Failed to reserve <DOMAIN>.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_416?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_416) and include the error code.
