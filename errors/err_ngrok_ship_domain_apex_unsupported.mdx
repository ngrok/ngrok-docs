## What does ERR_NGROK_SHIP_DOMAIN_APEX_UNSUPPORTED mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_SHIP_DOMAIN_APEX_UNSUPPORTED with this message:

```text
<PROVIDER> cannot point a root domain at ngrok. It serves no ALIAS, ANAME, or flattened CNAME record.
```
