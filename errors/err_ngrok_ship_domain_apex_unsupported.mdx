## ERR_NGROK_SHIP_DOMAIN_APEX_UNSUPPORTED

{/* vale off */}
`PROVIDER` cannot point a root domain at ngrok. It serves no ALIAS, ANAME, or flattened CNAME record.
{/* vale on */}
