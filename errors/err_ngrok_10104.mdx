## What does ERR_NGROK_10104 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_10104 with this message:

```text
Signup domains aren't available on this account. Contact ngrok support at support@ngrok.com to enable them.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_10104?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_10104) and include the error code.
