## ERR_NGROK_26004

{/* vale off */}
CAA records for domain `DOMAIN` found at `FOUNDAT` restrict validation methods to `ALLOWEDMETHOD`, but `REQUIREDMETHOD` is required for this domain type.
{/* vale on */}
