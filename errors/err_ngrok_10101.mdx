## What does ERR_NGROK_10101 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_10101 with this message:

```text
Signup domains need the Identity & Access Governance Suite add-on. Add it to your plan at https://dashboard.ngrok.com/legacy/billing/subscription/add-ons, then request your domain again.
```
