## What does ERR_NGROK_18010 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_18010 with this message:

```text
You have exceeded the maximum number of cloud endpoints allowed for this account. Log into https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing/choose-a-plan to view your subscription options.
```
