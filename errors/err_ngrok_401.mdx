## What does ERR_NGROK_401 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_401 with this message:

```text
Only accounts on paid plans can reserve domains.
Your account can't reserve domains. 

Upgrade to a paid plan at: https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing/choose-a-plan
```
