## What does ERR_NGROK_27246 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_27246 with this message:

```text
Your account already holds <USEDBYTES> of the <LIMITBYTES> an ngrok-managed key allows for provider "<PROVIDERID>". Delete files you no longer need, or add your own provider key.
```
