## What does ERR_NGROK_314 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_314 with this message:

```text
Only paid plans may create endpoints with custom hostnames.
Failed to create an endpoint with the custom hostname <HOSTNAME> for the account <ACCOUNT>.
This account is on the <PLAN> plan.

Upgrade to a paid plan at: https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing/choose-a-plan
```

<Tip>
Troubleshoot ERR_NGROK_314 with your AI agent.
Connect it to the [ngrok MCP server](/mcp-server), then ask what caused the error and how to fix it.
</Tip>

## How do I fix ERR_NGROK_314?

- Upgrade your account to a Pro or Pay-as-you-go plan from your [account dashboard](https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing/choose-a-plan) to reserve a custom hostname.

<Tip>
If you don't need a fully custom hostname, you can reserve a custom ngrok domain (like `example.ngrok.app`) on a paid plan.
</Tip>
