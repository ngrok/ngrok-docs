## What does ERR_NGROK_5325 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_5325 with this message:

```text
Failed to complete IdP-initiated SAML login. Edge does not have any attached domains. Ensure that at least one domain is configured as a hostport for "<EDGID>".
```
