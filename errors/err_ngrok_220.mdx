## What does ERR_NGROK_220 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_220 with this message:

```text
Your IP does not match the IP Policy for this Account.  Authenticate from an IP in the correct range or update your IP Restrictions from the ngrok dashboard. https://dashboard.ngrok.com/ip-restrictions
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_220?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_220) and include the error code.
