## What does ERR_NGROK_744 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_744 with this message:

```text
This ngrok account has reached its TCP connections limit for the minute. It resets within a minute. Log into https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing to view your options.
```
