## What does ERR_NGROK_6033 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_6033 with this message:

```text
Insuffiucient AI Gateway Credits - Your credit balance is too low to use AI Gateway. You can purchase credits from the dashboard: https://dashboard.ngrok.com/ai-gateways . If you've already upgraded, the change may take a few minutes to propagate, please try again in a little bit. If you are still experiencing this issue after 20-30 minutes, please contact support.
```
