## What does ERR_NGROK_501 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_501 with this message:

```text
Only paid accounts can reserve addresses.
Your account can't reserve addresses. 

Upgrade to a paid plan at https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing/choose-a-plan
```
