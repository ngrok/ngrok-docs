## ERR_NGROK_DASH_SESSION_LIFETIME_NOT_ALLOWED

{/* vale off */}
Dashboard session lifetime needs the Identity & Access Governance Suite add-on. Add it to your plan at https://dashboard.ngrok.com/legacy/billing/subscription/add-ons, then set the lifetime again.
{/* vale on */}
