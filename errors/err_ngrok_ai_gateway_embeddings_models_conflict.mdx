## What does ERR_NGROK_AI_GATEWAY_EMBEDDINGS_MODELS_CONFLICT mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_AI_GATEWAY_EMBEDDINGS_MODELS_CONFLICT with this message:

```text
An embeddings request can fail over only between providers of one model. <MODEL> and <OTHERMODEL> are different models, so their vectors aren't comparable.
```
