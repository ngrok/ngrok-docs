## ERR_NGROK_SHIP_PUBLISH_TARGET_KIND_UNSUPPORTED

{/* vale off */}
The 'kind' value `KIND` is not supported. Allowed: 'builtin', 'ghcr', 'dockerhub', and 'custom'.
{/* vale on */}
