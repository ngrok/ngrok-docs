## What does ERR_NGROK_205 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_205 with this message:

```text
The account <ACCOUNT> has been suspended. This is usually the result of violating the ngrok Terms of Service. Email support@ngrok.com if you think your suspension is an error.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_205?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_205) and include the error code.
