## What does ERR_NGROK_327 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_327 with this message:

```text
Domain has an invalid character sequence. Valid domains may not contain repeated periods '..'. Failed to create an endpoint with the domain <DOMAIN>.
```

Troubleshoot ERR_NGROK_327 with your AI agent.

</Tip>
