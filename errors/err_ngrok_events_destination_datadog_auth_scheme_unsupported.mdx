## What does ERR_NGROK_EVENTS_DESTINATION_DATADOG_AUTH_SCHEME_UNSUPPORTED mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_EVENTS_DESTINATION_DATADOG_AUTH_SCHEME_UNSUPPORTED with this message:

```text
The Datadog auth_scheme <SCHEME> is not supported. Use 'dd_api_key' or 'bearer'.
```
