## ERR_NGROK_EVENTS_DESTINATION_DATADOG_AUTH_SCHEME_UNSUPPORTED

{/* vale off */}
The Datadog auth_scheme `SCHEME` is not supported. Use 'dd_api_key' or 'bearer'.
{/* vale on */}
