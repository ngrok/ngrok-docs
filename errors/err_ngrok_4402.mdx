## What does ERR_NGROK_4402 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_4402 with this message:

```text
The invited user would exceed your current team size limit of <TEAM_SIZE>. Add another seat at: https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing/choose-a-plan. Then you'll be able to invite your team.
```
