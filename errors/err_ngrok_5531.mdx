## What does ERR_NGROK_5531 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_5531 with this message:

```text
<PROVIDER> denied access to your account as the correct account could not be chosen automatically.

Try authorizing again or contacting the owner of this application if the issue persists.
```
