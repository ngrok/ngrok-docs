## What does ERR_NGROK_1011 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_1011 with this message:

```text
Your account is currently past due. You may only enter a payment method to pay your overdue invoice or switch to the free plan.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_1011?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_1011) and include the error code.
