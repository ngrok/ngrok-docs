## What does ERR_NGROK_10099 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_10099 with this message:

```text
SSO is not authorized for this email domain. Contact your administrator or ngrok support if you believe this is a mistake.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_10099?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_10099) and include the error code.
