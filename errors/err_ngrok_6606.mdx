## What does ERR_NGROK_6606 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_6606 with this message:

```text
You may not change an administrator's permissions. Only other administrators may make that change.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_6606?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_6606) and include the error code.
