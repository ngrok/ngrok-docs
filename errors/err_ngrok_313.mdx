## What does ERR_NGROK_313 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_313 with this message:

```text
Only paid plans may create endpoints with custom subdomains.
Failed to create an endpoint with the custom subdomain <SUBDOMAIN> for the account <ACCOUNT>.
This account is on the <PLAN> plan.

Upgrade to a paid plan at: https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing/choose-a-plan
```
