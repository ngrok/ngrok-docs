## ERR_NGROK_5319

{/* vale off */}
Failed to complete IdP-initiated SAML login. The RelayState specified an invalid domain "`DOMAIN`" to redirect to. Ensure that the domain "`DOMAIN`" is configured as a hostport for "`EDGID`".
{/* vale on */}
