## What does ERR_NGROK_1108 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_1108 with this message:

```text
<CMD> is not a valid tunnel type. The SSH command must be one of 'http', 'tls', or 'tcp'.
```

Troubleshoot ERR_NGROK_1108 with your AI agent.
