## What does ERR_NGROK_622 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_622 with this message:

```text
Only Pay-as-you-go plans can use SSH ACL rules.
Your account is not authorized to use SSH ACL rules.

Upgrade to a Pay-as-you-go plan at: https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing/choose-a-plan?plan=paygo
```
