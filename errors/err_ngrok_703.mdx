## ERR_NGROK_703

{/* vale off */}
Too many connections! The account hosting this endpoint has violated the rate-limit policy of `THRESHOLD` connections per minute by initiating `COUNT` connections in the last `SECONDS` seconds. Decrease your inbound connection volume or upgrade your account plan for additional capacity.
{/* vale on */}
