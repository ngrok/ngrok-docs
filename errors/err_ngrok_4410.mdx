## What does ERR_NGROK_4410 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_4410 with this message:

```text
You do not have permissions to invite an administrator to the account. Only administrators may invite other administrators.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_4410?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_4410) and include the error code.
