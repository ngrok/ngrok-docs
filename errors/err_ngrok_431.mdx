## What does ERR_NGROK_431 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_431 with this message:

```text
Your account is limited to <MAX> reserved domains. If you need more domains, you can upgrade to a Pay-as-you-go plan at: https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing/choose-a-plan?plan=paygo.
```
