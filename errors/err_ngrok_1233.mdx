## What does ERR_NGROK_1233 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_1233 with this message:

```text
The email address <EMAIL> is already associated with an SSO Identity Provider connection. Contact support: support@ngrok.com
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_1233?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_1233) and include the error code.
