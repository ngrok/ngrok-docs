## What does ERR_NGROK_SHIP_ENVIRONMENT_TASK_ROLE_ACCOUNT_NOT_ALLOWED mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_SHIP_ENVIRONMENT_TASK_ROLE_ACCOUNT_NOT_ALLOWED with this message:

```text
The 'task_role_arn' role must be in the AWS account of pool <ID>. IAM cannot pass a role from another account.
```
