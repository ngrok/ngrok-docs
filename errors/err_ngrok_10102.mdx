## ERR_NGROK_10102
Signup domains send new users to your SSO identity provider. Set one up at https://dashboard.ngrok.com/settings/auth, then request your domain again.




## Further help
If you're having trouble resolving this error, please reach out to [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_10102)
