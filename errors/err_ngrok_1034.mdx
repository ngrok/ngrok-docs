## What does ERR_NGROK_1034 mean?

ngrok returns ERR_NGROK_1034 with this message:

```text
Your payment for <MSG> has not settled yet. Your account is restored automatically once it clears, so no further action is needed.
```




## Where can I get more help with ERR_NGROK_1034?

Email [support@ngrok.com](mailto:support@ngrok.com?subject=Help%20with%20ERR_NGROK_1034) and include the error code.
