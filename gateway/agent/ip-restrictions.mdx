---
title: Restrict Where Agents Connect From
sidebarTitle: IP Restrictions
description: Restrict which IP addresses and CIDR ranges are allowed to open an agent session on your ngrok account, on top of authtoken authentication.
---

Agents that authenticate with an authtoken can also be restricted with IP
Policies that specify which IPs and CIDRs they may connect to your ngrok account
from.
The [Reverse SSH Agent](/gateway/agent/ssh-reverse-tunnel-agent/#authentication)
authenticates with an SSH public key instead.

- [IP Restrictions on your ngrok dashboard](https://dashboard.ngrok.com/ip-restrictions)
- [IP Restrictions API Resource](/api-reference/iprestrictions/list/)
