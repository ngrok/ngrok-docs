title: Credits

AI Gateway credits are a prepaid balance you purchase through [app.ngrok.ai](https://app.ngrok.ai). Every request through `https://gateway.ngrok.ai` spends credits.

Each request pays a small [processing fee](#processing-fee). When you pay with credits, credits also cover the model cost. See [How credits are consumed](#how-credits-are-consumed) for the full breakdown.

AI Gateway credits are separate from your ngrok platform billing on [dashboard.ngrok.com](https://dashboard.ngrok.com).

## Accounts and plans

You don't need a specific ngrok subscription plan to use the AI Gateway or purchase credits. Sign in at [app.ngrok.ai](https://app.ngrok.ai), buy credits, and create an [access key](/ai-gateway/concepts/access-keys).

The AI Gateway and your broader ngrok account share the same ngrok account, but they are separate systems:

| | [app.ngrok.ai](https://app.ngrok.ai) | [dashboard.ngrok.com](https://dashboard.ngrok.com) |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | AI Gateway: keys, providers, credits, usage | ngrok platform: subscription, team members, tunnels, endpoints |
| **Credits** | AI Gateway credits (this page) | Not used for AI Gateway traffic |

If you want ngrok platform features alongside the AI Gateway (such as team members, or [internal endpoints](/ai-gateway/concepts/custom-providers) to reach self-hosted models) you can manage those through [dashboard.ngrok.com](https://dashboard.ngrok.com). This generally requires a paid ngrok plan such as [Pay-as-you-go](https://ngrok.com/pricing); using the gateway with credits alone does not.

## Purchasing credits

Visit the [Billing page](https://app.ngrok.ai/billing) and purchase at least USD \$5.00. Your balance can't go above USD \$500.00, so a purchase that would take it over the cap is rejected.

You can also buy credits with the AI Gateway API. The purchase charges the default card saved on your account, so save a card on the Billing page first:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.ngrok.ai/credits/purchases \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)" \
  -d '{ "amount": 500 }'
```

The response returns a purchase with `status: pending`. Poll [`GET /credits/purchases/{id}`](/ai-gateway/api-reference/credits/purchase-get) until it reaches `succeeded` or `failed`. If the card issuer asks for 3D Secure approval, the status becomes `actionRequired` and `completionUrl` links the page where you approve the payment.

Send a new `Idempotency-Key` for each purchase so a retried request never charges you twice.

<Note>
Some older accounts are billed through Stripe Checkout instead. For those accounts, the purchases endpoint returns `metronome_not_provisioned`; use [`POST /credits/checkout-session`](/ai-gateway/api-reference/credits/checkout-session) instead.
</Note>

Credits expire **one year** after purchase.

## Automatic top-up

Automatic top-up adds credits from your saved card when your balance runs low.

To set it up:

1. Open the [Billing page](https://app.ngrok.ai/billing) and make sure you have a saved card.
2. Select **Enable auto top-up**, then turn on **Enable auto top-up** in the dialog.
3. Set **When your balance reaches** (at least \$5) and **Top up to** (up to \$500).
4. Optional: select **Set a spending limit** and enter a **Maximum spend every billing period**.
5. Select **Save changes**.

## How credits are consumed

| Routing | Credits pay for | You pay separately |
|---------|-----------------|------------------|
| Built-in provider, paid with credits | Processing fee and the provider's model cost, including hosted tools such as web search, code execution, and image generation | Nothing |
| Built-in provider with your provider key | Processing fee | Upstream model cost to your provider |

## Balance tracking

You can view your balance on the [Billing page](https://app.ngrok.ai/billing), or via the API:

```bash
curl https://api.ngrok.ai/credits/balance \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY"
```

## What happens when credits run out

When your credit balance reaches zero, the gateway rejects new requests with HTTP 402 and [`ERR_NGROK_27206`](/ai-gateway/reference/error-codes#err_ngrok_27206) until you purchase more credits. That's true even if you use your own provider keys, since every request pays a small processing fee from your credits.

To avoid this, turn on [automatic top-up](#automatic-top-up) so your balance refills before it runs out.

The balance updates shortly after usage, so requests already in flight can take it slightly below zero. The [balance API](/ai-gateway/api-reference/credits/balance) reports a negative value when that happens.

## Processing fee

Every successful request through the gateway incurs a processing fee at **USD $0.05 per million tokens**. The fee is based on the tokens in your request and response. It applies to all traffic—requests paid with credits, your own [provider keys](/ai-gateway/guides/attaching-provider-keys), and [custom providers](/ai-gateway/concepts/custom-providers)—and does not vary by model or provider.

When you pay with credits, credits also cover the provider's model cost. When you bring your own key, model costs stay on your provider account and credits cover only the processing fee.
