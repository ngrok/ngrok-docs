Navigate to the [**Domains** section](https://dashboard.ngrok.com/domains) of the ngrok dashboard and click **New +** to reserve a free static domain like {<code>{domain}</code> || `https://your-service.ngrok.app`} or a [custom domain](/gateway/domains/custom-domains/) you already own.

This guide refers to this domain as `$NGROK_DOMAIN` from here on out.
