Navigate to the [**Domains** section](https://dashboard.ngrok.com/domains) of the ngrok dashboard and click **New +** to reserve a free static domain like <code>{domain || "https://your-service.ngrok.app"}</code> or a [custom domain](/gateway/domains/custom-domains/) you already own.

This guide refers to this domain as `$NGROK_DOMAIN` from here on out.
