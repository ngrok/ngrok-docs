ngrok is developer infrastructure that routes and secures traffic to your apps, APIs, and AI models.
It manages connectivity between all of the tools and platforms you use to put your services online, no matter where they're deployed or what network they're on.

ngrok's core products are Gateway, AI Gateway, and Share Localhost.
Gateway owns the connectivity primitives, and the other two products integrate them rather than reimplementing them.

## What can you do with ngrok?

### Build & test

- **[Share a local app](/guides/share-localhost/overview)**: Put anything on localhost behind a stable, secure public URL.
- **[Test webhooks locally](/guides/share-localhost/webhooks)**: Inspect, replay, and verify requests while you build.
- **[Connect local MCP servers](/using-ngrok-with/using-mcp)**: Let ChatGPT and Claude reach tools on your machine.

### Play

- **[Host a Minecraft server](/gateway/examples/minecraft)**: Let your friends join, and block the griefers, without port forwarding.

### Deploy & run

- **[Route traffic to self-hosted models](/ai-gateway/guides/use-a-model-you-run-yourself)**: Privately connect to any hardware, in any network, over an AI gateway.
- **[Run preview and CI workloads](/gateway/examples/ephemeral-workloads)**: Give every ephemeral service a secure public URL.

### Deliver & connect

- **[Deliver and secure APIs](/gateway/api-gateway/get-started)**: Route, authenticate, and protect requests through an API gateway.
- **[Reach customer networks](/gateway/site-to-site-connectivity/overview)**: Connect to customer APIs and databases with site-to-site connectivity.
- **[Receive webhooks on-prem](/gateway/webhooks-to-on-prem/overview)**: Verify signatures for 70+ providers at one webhook gateway.
- **[Connect privately across networks](/gateway/endpoints/internal-endpoints)**: Reach across network boundaries without public URLs, open ports, or VPNs.
- **[Connect device fleets](/gateway/device-gateway/overview)**: Reach devices in the field through a device gateway, no inbound ports.
- **[Open SSH and RDP sessions](/gateway/ssh-rdp)**: Reach a shell or desktop on a remote machine without a VPN.

## Gateway primitives

Gateway owns the primitives that move traffic.
AI Gateway and Share Localhost build on them, so what you learn once applies wherever you use ngrok.

- [Endpoints](/gateway/endpoints/): The URLs traffic arrives on, whether created by an agent or in the cloud.
- [Traffic Policy](/gateway/traffic-policy/): Filter, match, manage, and orchestrate traffic to your endpoints.
- [Domains & TLS](/gateway/domains/): Serve traffic on your own hostnames, and manage the certificates that terminate TLS.

## Account-wide

These apply across every product in your account.

- [Traffic observability](/obs/): Capture and replay requests and responses.
- [Identity and access](/iam/): Manage credentials for human users and automated processes.
