ngrok is developer infrastructure that routes and secures traffic to your apps, APIs, and AI models.
It manages connectivity between all of the tools and platforms you use to put your services online, no matter where they're deployed or what network they're on.

ngrok's core products are Gateway, AI Gateway, and Share Localhost.
Shared building blocks like the ngrok agent, Traffic Policy, and traffic observability work the same way across all three.

## What can you do with ngrok?

### Production ingress

- **API Gateway**: Use [Traffic Policy](/gateway/traffic-policy/actions/) to secure, protect, accelerate, and transform traffic to your production APIs.
- **Kubernetes ingress**: Run the [ngrok Kubernetes Operator](/k8s/) to create ingress to services in any cluster.
- **Identity-aware proxy**: Federate your app's authentication to an identity provider with the [OAuth](/gateway/traffic-policy/actions/oauth/), [JWT validation](/gateway/traffic-policy/actions/jwt-validation/), and [OpenID Connect](/gateway/traffic-policy/actions/oidc/) actions.
- **Load balancer**: Use [endpoint pooling](/gateway/endpoints/endpoint-pooling/) for scalability, failover, and blue/green or canary deployments.

### Ingress to external networks

- **APIs in customer networks**: Run the lightweight [ngrok agent](/agent/) or [Kubernetes Operator](/k8s/) in your customers' environments to reach APIs in their networks without complex network configuration.
- **APIs on devices**: Run [ngrok as a service](/agent/#running-ngrok-in-the-background) on your devices so your cloud service can control and administrate them.
- **APIs in local dev environments**: Import [ngrok as a library](/agent-sdks) into your own CLI to build better local dev experiences for your developer customers.

### Development and testing

- **Webhook testing**: Receive webhooks directly in the app you're developing, then [inspect and replay requests](/agent/web-inspection-interface/).
- **Local previews**: Demo a site running on your machine to a client or stakeholder without deploying to staging.
- **Mobile backend testing**: Test mobile apps against a backend running on your local machine.

### Remote access

- **SSH and RDP**: Create [TCP endpoints](/gateway/endpoints/tcp/) to reach remote machines.
- **MCP servers**: Let ChatGPT, Claude, and other AI clients talk to an MCP server running on your laptop.

## Shared building blocks

These work the same way across every ngrok product.

- [Endpoints](/gateway/endpoints/) - The URLs traffic arrives on, whether created by an agent or in the cloud.
- [Traffic Policy](/gateway/traffic-policy/) - Filter, match, manage, and orchestrate traffic to your endpoints.
- [Traffic observability](/obs/) - Capture and replay requests and responses.
- [Identity and access](/iam/) - Manage credentials for human users and automated processes.
- [ngrok agent](/agent/) - Connect local services and devices to ngrok's global network.
- [Kubernetes Operator](/k8s/) - Automate endpoint creation for services in your cluster.
