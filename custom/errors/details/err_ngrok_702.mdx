## Why does ngrok return ERR_NGROK_702?

- ngrok limits the number of inbound connections to your tunnels.
- ngrok limits connections, not requests.
If your HTTP clients use persistent connections, also known as HTTP keep-alive (most modern ones do), you'll likely never hit this limit.
- ngrok returns a `429` response to HTTP connections that exceed the rate limit.
- ngrok closes connections to TCP and TLS tunnels that violate the rate limit without a response.

## How do I fix ERR_NGROK_702?

- Reduce the number of new inbound connections, for example by having clients reuse persistent connections.
- Upgrade to a paid plan from your [account dashboard](https://dashboard.ngrok.com/billing/choose-a-plan) for a higher connection rate limit.
See [Pricing and limits](/pricing-limits) for each plan's limits.
