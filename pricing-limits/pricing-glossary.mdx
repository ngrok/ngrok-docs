Definitions for every feature and metric that appears in the [ngrok pricing docs](/pricing-limits/).

## Online endpoints

The number of endpoints you can have online at the same time.

[Learn More](/gateway/endpoints/agent-endpoints/)

## Development endpoint hours

Public endpoints started with your development domain don't accrue endpoint hours.

[Learn More](/pricing-limits/#limits-and-licensing)

## Active endpoint hours

An endpoint is active if it has outgoing traffic during the hour.

[Learn More](/pricing-limits/#limits-and-licensing)

## Endpoint protocols

The protocols available on each plan.

[Learn More](/gateway/endpoints/agent-endpoints#protocols%2C-binding-and-pooling)

## Endpoint hours

The amount of time your endpoints are online.

## Load balancing

Load balancing at ngrok is called endpoint pooling.

[Learn More](/gateway/endpoints/endpoint-pooling)

## Domains

Domains you own and register with ngrok.

[Learn More](/gateway/domains/)

## Development domain

Your development domain is specific to your account, and doesn't incur usage charges.
Endpoints on it are free within your account's limits.

[Learn More](/gateway/domains/)

## ngrok-branded domains

Use any ngrok-branded domain that you pick from ngrok's pool.

[Learn More](/gateway/domains/)

## Traffic Policy Units

Traffic Policy Units (TPUs) are ngrok's usage-based metric for measuring the work your Traffic Policies perform.

[Learn More](/pricing-limits/traffic-policy-unit-pricing)

## Bring your own custom domains

Use any custom domain name that you already own with ngrok.

[Learn More](/gateway/domains/custom-domains/)

## Wildcard Domains

You can create an endpoint that receives traffic for all of the subdomains matching a wildcard domain like `*.example.com`.

[Learn More](/gateway/endpoints/http/#wildcard-endpoints)

## TCP Addresses

TCP Addresses enable you to create public TCP Endpoints on a fixed address.

[Learn More](/gateway/domains/tcp-addresses/)

## Data transfer out

The total volume of data transferred outbound from ngrok's network to clients, including traffic forwarded to agents.

[Learn More](/pricing-limits/#limits-and-licensing)

## Requests to HTTP/S endpoints

The maximum number of HTTP/S requests a client can make to an account's endpoints in a month.

[Learn More](/gateway/endpoints/http/)

## Connections to TCP/TLS endpoints

The maximum number of TCP/TLS connections a client can make to an account's endpoints in a month.

[Learn More](/gateway/endpoints/tcp/)

## HTTP Requests

The maximum number of HTTP requests across all endpoints per minute.

[Learn More](/pricing-limits/how-ngrok-charges/)

## TCP Connections

The maximum number of TCP connections across all endpoints per minute.

[Learn More](/pricing-limits/how-ngrok-charges/)

## Bring your own certificates

Upload your own TLS certificates if you don't want to use the TLS certificates that ngrok automatically provisions for you.

[Learn More](/gateway/endpoints/tls/)

## End-to-End TLS

Terminate TLS at your upstream service or at the ngrok agent to achieve end-to-end encryption.

[Learn More](/gateway/domains/tls-termination/)

## Mutual TLS

Mutual TLS (mTLS) is a protocol in which the client and server authenticate each other with certificates.

[Learn More](/gateway/traffic-policy/)

## Traffic Policy Units (TPUs)

The actions and variables applied to a request.
Features such as WAF and mTLS consume TPUs.

[Learn More](/pricing-limits/traffic-policy-unit-pricing/)

## Traffic Identities

The number of end users who authenticate to your app or service through a Traffic Policy action, such as OAuth, SAML, or OIDC.

[Learn More](/gateway/traffic-policy/)

## Traffic Inspector Retention

The number of hours ngrok retains your traffic data in Traffic Inspector.

[Learn More](/obs/traffic-inspection)

## Traffic Log Exporting

Export event logs for traffic through your endpoints to S3, Datadog, Azure Logs, CloudWatch Logs, and more.

[Learn More](/obs/)

## Concurrent Agents

The maximum number of ngrok agents that can be simultaneously connected to the ngrok cloud service under a single account.

[Learn More](/gateway/agent/)

## Dedicated Agent Connect IPs

Get a constant, dedicated IP for your account's agents.

[Learn More](/gateway/agent/)

## Custom Agent Connect URLs

Customize the URL that the agent connects to.

[Learn More](/gateway/agent/)

## Remote Agent Update Operations

Remotely stop, restart, or update ngrok agents running as a service.

[Learn More](/gateway/agent/)

## Users

Members of your account who can view or create endpoints.

[Learn More](/iam/users/)

## Service Users

Service users are accounts for automated systems that programmatically interact with your ngrok accounts by starting ngrok agents or making requests to the API.

[Learn More](/iam/service-users/)

## SSO/RBAC

Federate authentication to your identity provider (IdP) with SAML or OpenID Connect.

[Learn More](/iam/sso/)

## Identity and Access Governance Suite

Includes SCIM provisioning, account-wide IP restrictions, and audit logs.

[Learn More](/iam/)

## Domain Controls

Claim ownership of an email domain and control which accounts users with that domain can create or join.
Domain Controls aren't self-serve, so contact ngrok to enable them on your account.

[Learn More](/iam/domain-controls/)

## Authtoken ACLs

Authtoken ACLs restrict which endpoints an ngrok agent can create when using that authtoken.

[Learn More](/gateway/agent/config/)

## Basic support

Email support and best-effort response times.

## Slack and MS Teams

A dedicated Slack or Microsoft Teams channel with a 24-hour response SLA.

## Dedicated On-Call

Contractual uptime and support SLAs.

## Region-specific routing

Configure your domains to route traffic only through specific geographic regions.

## HIPAA / BAAs

Self-serve Pay-as-you-go add-on: enable it from the dashboard billing page and sign the BAA yourself.
Usage-based charges cost 50% more.

## SOC 2

Independent verification that your data is secure.

## Security questionnaires

If your team needs a security questionnaire, ngrok can prepare it.

## Invoicing

If your team needs an invoice for billing purposes, ngrok can send it.
